# Brag Plan: Caring for Your Dental Implants

## What is this app?
A static, bilingual patient-education page for the implant care video: one clear message, a scene-by-scene guide, a clinical accuracy checklist, pronunciation help and viewer test questions.

## The angle
Quiet and trustworthy, not flashy. The video is for older adults, so the brag is clarity: one message, shown calmly.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The Arabic label "العناية بزراعة الأسنان" over the teal hero gradient, then the English title "Caring for Your Dental Implants" fades in below it.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The one message: "daily cleaning protects your implant" (Arabic and English, large and centered).
- Three care steps as three calm cards: brush twice a day, clean between implants with an interdental brush, see your dentist for warning signs.

## Outro / punchline
"See your dentist regularly" and a closing line: "Care for your implant, and it will last." Then the title card again.

## User flow worth showing
none — landing-page only. The strongest material is the scene cards and the one message.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: calm, clear, trustworthy, not flashy; built for older adults (user-provided)
- Interpretation: long holds, large high-contrast type, slow cross-dissolves, no fast zooms, no spins.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #f7f9f9
- Accent: #0f7c7c (teal), hero gradient #0f7c7c to #3d8fb8
- Text: #1d2a2c
- Display font: system-ui (page fallback); Arabic labels intended for Tajawal or Cairo, which the page does not load
- Body font: system-ui
- Strongest visual element: the teal hero band and the numbered scene cards

## Share copy (draft)
Daily cleaning protects your dental implant. A short, calm guide in Arabic and English.

## Audio direction
- Role: intentional silence or a very low bed (no voice narration, as requested)
- Music: none (default choice for a calm, clinical message; change if you want a soft bed)
- Music treatment: n/a
- Music cue guidance: n/a (music off)
- Audio-reactive treatment: none
- SFX posture: none
- Audio-coupled moments: none
- Restraint rule: no sound under the warning-signs card
